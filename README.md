# Features

## 🗺️ Build Planner — Path of Exile 2

Bring your Path of Building builds directly into Path of Exile 2.

Poe Lurker bridges the gap between Path of Building and the official in-game Build Planner, making it easier than ever to follow your favorite builds without constantly switching between applications.

- **Path of Building integration** — Automatically discover your locally saved builds.
- **Native Build Planner integration** — Convert your Path of Building builds into the official Path of Exile 2 Build Planner format.
- **Character-specific builds** — Assign a different build to each of your characters.
- **Automatic synchronization** — Keep your in-game build up to date when your Path of Building build changes.
- **Passive skill tree** — Follow your planned passive skill tree directly in-game.
- **Item recommendations** — Access your build's recommended items.
- **Skill and support gems** — Easily visualize the gems associated with your build.
- **In-game overlay** — Manage your builds without leaving the game.

## 💎 Build-Specific Loot Filters — Path of Exile 2

Your loot filter should adapt to your build, not the other way around.

Poe Lurker allows you to extend your favorite loot filter with additional rules tailored specifically to your build.

- Choose a base loot filter, such as NeverSink's Loot Filter.
- Add custom filter conditions on top of your base filter.
- Prioritize specific weapons, equipment, and crafting bases relevant to your build.
- Manage your additional filter rules directly from the overlay.
- Maintain a dedicated filter configuration for each build.
- Enable or disable build-specific filtering whenever you want.
- Preserve your original base filter without modifying it.

For example, if you're playing a two-handed mace build, you can highlight relevant two-handed maces and crafting bases without sacrificing the benefits of your existing loot filter.

**Your build. Your loot. Your rules.**

## 💰 Trade Management

Simplify your trading experience with an intuitive and non-intrusive in-game overlay.

- Incoming and outgoing trade management.
- Quick access to common trade interactions.
- Streamlined communication with buyers and sellers.
- Less time managing trades and more time playing.

# Installation

- Install the [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet/10.0).
- Download and run [`PoeLurkerSetup.exe`](https://github.com/C1rdec/Poe-Lurker/releases/latest/download/PoeLurkerSetup.exe).

> **Upgrading from Poe Lurker 1.x?**
>
> Poe Lurker 2.0 has been migrated from .NET Framework 4.8 to .NET 10 and uses a new installer framework. If you encounter any issues while updating, uninstall your previous version of Poe Lurker and install the latest release.
